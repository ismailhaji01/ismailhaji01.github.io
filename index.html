Payment Gateway

One integration, every way your customers pay.

Businesses connect once and collect from M-Pesa, Mixx by Yas, Airtel Money and HaloPesa, take bank and card payments through CRDB, NBC and NMB, and send payouts. When a network doesn't send back a result, the gateway asks for the status and retries.

Power Bank Rental System

Rent a charged power bank from a station and bring it back when you're done.

Customers sign in with a one-time SMS code, unlock a power bank from a smart cabinet and pay with mobile money or cash. The system talks to the cabinets through their API, sends return reminders and overdue alerts by SMS, and splits revenue with the shops that host each cabinet.
