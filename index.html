Preface

A note before the work

I lead the engineering team at EvMak. We run EvPay, a gateway that connects merchants to M-Pesa, Mixx by Yas, Airtel Money, HaloPesa and three banks through one API.

Most of my week goes on the parts customers never see: the callback that arrives late, the status query that recovers it, and the reconciliation report that has to match the network's statement at the end of the day. I write that code in Laravel and Go and run it on Linux servers I look after myself.

I studied cyber security and digital forensics at the University of Dodoma and spent a few months examining phones in cybercrime cases at Police Headquarters. Since then, I look at how an integration could be abused before I ship it.

Lately I'm also building an Android app for a POS terminal that takes payment from a scan of your palm, and middleware that sends vehicle tracking data to LATRA.

EvPay Payment Gateway

Merchants call one endpoint and EvPay routes the payment to the right network or bank. Each provider has its own module: Vodacom, Tigo, Airtel, Halotel, CRDB, NBC and NMB.

It handles USSD push, C2B, biller payments, payouts, bank control numbers and card payments. When a network doesn't send a callback, a queued job asks it for the status and retries.

Private repo · PHP 8.2 · SOAP and REST · Supervisor

LATRA vehicle data

Trucks and buses carry tracking devices. Our middleware collects what those devices send and forwards it to LATRA, the Land Transport Regulatory Authority.

I built it with InnovaTECH Solutions Tanzania Limited, who supply the telematics and fleet systems for transport, logistics, mining and oil and gas companies.
